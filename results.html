Step 05 — Security Results

Here's what actually happened.

No score, no points — just a plain explanation of what happened, why, and which controls actually mattered. At the end, we'll show you exactly what gave the phishing message away.

It looks like you reached this page without going through the simulated scenario and login pages, so the recap below may be empty. Start from the landing page →

No single control in this simulation could have stopped a phishing message from being convincing — that's a human judgment call, not a network setting. VPN and Firewall protect different things (traffic privacy and suspicious outbound activity) that matter for other kinds of threats, even on scenarios where neither stopped the credential theft. MFA is what actually limited the damage once credentials were already gone. Layered security means every layer still matters — just not always for the reason you'd expect.
